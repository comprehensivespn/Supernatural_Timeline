# Inside TV: Secrets of 'Supernatural'

Scanned page posted by Simone at the sn_daly community (livejournal) 23/11/2005. Also referenced by the supernatural_newsletter 24/11/2005.

The scanneda article shows images from the Home episode. Dating it, based on the images from most likely after the second week of October, since they were filming the episode then and it would not make sense for the images to be included on an article of the same week given weekly publication. 

The article itself was published according to the scanned copied on the 31 of october week. Making it the issue 31 october-6 november. 

You can find the images in the article among raloria's posts about season one on the spn_ontheset community (livejournal)

The article reads:

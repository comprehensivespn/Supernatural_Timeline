# Airing of a promotional snippet

During the Sunday Special of that day a 4 minute preview of Supernatural seemed to take place. 

05/08/2005 Filmobsessed137 commented on the acklesaholics livejournal community:

"I was sitting here, with the WB in the background, and they advertised that Sunday they will be airing the movie Sugar & Spice and at some point during the movie there will be a 4 minute preview of Supernatural. Just wanted to let everyone know so they can check it out."

The WB archived schedule for that week confirms the movie. Since the page with more information on the special was not archive it is not possible to know when exactly or what part of the episode may have been the preview. 

My best guess is that the preview was either the scene were Dean is introduced or the 22 years before one.  

I tried checking commercial breaks on The WB that may have adverstised the preview but I could not find any.
